Privacy Policy

This OAuth application is used solely by its owner for a personal rclone installation.

Google Drive access

The application accesses Google Drive solely for the purpose of uploading, listing, managing, and deleting backup files created by the owner's rclone installation.

Data collection

No personal information is collected for advertising, analytics, profiling, or other commercial purposes.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred, or shared with third parties. Data is transferred only between the owner's server and the owner's Google Drive account.

Credentials

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's server and are not intentionally shared with third parties.
